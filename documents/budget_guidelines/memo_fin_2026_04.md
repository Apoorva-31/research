# EXECUTIVE FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM: CONTINGENCY BUFFER FREEZE
**Document ID:** MEMO-FIN-2026-04  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 10 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** General Financial Planning Standard Policy (2025)

Effective immediately, all event budget allocations must freeze discretionary contingency reserves to **0%**. 

Coordinators must allocate 100% of available event funds directly to line-item expenditures (catering, staffing, security, venue) without adding a 10% unallocated safety margin. Total proposed expenses across all departments must equal or be strictly less than the total allocated budget limit.
