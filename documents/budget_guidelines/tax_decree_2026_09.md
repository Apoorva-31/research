# GOVERNMENT FINANCIAL DECREE: SERVICE TAX REVISION
**Document ID:** TAX-DECREE-2026-09  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 1 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** Previous Service Tax Rate (5%)

Pursuant to the national finance ministry update, all commercial event catering, venue service contracts, and staffing expenditures are subject to an updated service tax rate of **12%**.

Budget allocations must immediately incorporate the 12% tax rate in all financial summaries and cost projections.
