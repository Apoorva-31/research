# Title: Operational Budget Guidelines & Expenditure Caps
# Version: 2.0
# Status: Current
# Effective Date: 15 August 2026

## Expenditure Rules
- Total event spend must never exceed the assigned event budget limit (e.g., ₹2,00,000 standard or ₹1,60,000 under revised reduction orders).
- Catering costs are capped at the specified per-person threshold (Standard rate: ₹450/person).
- Staffing and security allocations must be fully funded prior to discretionary or decorative spend. If a budget cut occurs, catering and staffing must be proportionally scaled while preserving mandatory safety rules.
