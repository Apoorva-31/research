# HIGH-RISK VIP SUMMIT BUDGET & SECURITY DIRECTIVE
**Document ID:** VIP-SECURITY-POLICY-01  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 8 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** Standard Budget Allocation Ratios

For all events designated as High-Risk VIP Summits, total catering expenditure is strictly capped at **25% of the total budget** (e.g., maximum ₹75,000 on a ₹3,00,000 budget).

Reallocated funds must be directed to executive protection and specialized security coverage. Standard 40% catering budget allocation rules do not apply to VIP-designated events.
