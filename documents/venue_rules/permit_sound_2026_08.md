# MUNICIPAL OUTDOOR SOUND PERMIT & NOISE ORDINANCE
**Document ID:** PERMIT-SOUND-2026-08  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 20 September 2026  
**Supersedes:** General Venue Noise Guidelines

Outdoor sound amplified entertainment for lawn galas and outdoor courtyards must cease strictly at **20:00 (8:00 PM)**.

While general indoor venue noise limits permit activity until 22:00, outdoor public address systems, live outdoor music, and exterior speakers must be powered down at 20:00 or moved entirely indoors.
