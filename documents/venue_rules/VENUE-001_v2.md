# Document ID: VENUE-001
# Title: Main Hall Venue Policy & Safety Guidelines
# Version: 2.0
# Status: Current
# Effective Date: 1 October 2026

## Capacity & Occupancy Limits
- Maximum permitted attendance for the Main Hall is strictly **240 people**. 
- Any registration or proposed layout exceeding 240 attendees is classified as an unresolvable hard capacity violation and must be rejected or capped.

## Emergency Exits & Safety Rules
- All designated emergency exits (Exits A, B, and C) must remain completely unobstructed at all times.
- Any stage, seating, or equipment layout that blocks a designated exit must be immediately rejected and reconfigured.
- Venue staff retain the right to halt event operations if safety perimeters are compromised.
