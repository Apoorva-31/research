# STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING ADVISORY: STAGE WEIGHT LIMITS
**Document ID:** STRUCTURAL-ADVISORY-HALL-B  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 11 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** Hall B Standard Load Capacity

Due to subfloor reinforcement work in Hall B, elevated stage platforms and heavy truss assemblies must not exceed a total weight of **600kg**.

Blueprints specifying 800kg staging designs are deemed structurally non-compliant and must be replaced with lightweight modular alternatives.
