# CITY FIRE MARSHAL ORDINANCE: EMERGENCY EXIT RATIOS
**Document ID:** FIRE-SAFETY-ORD-2026  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 15 September 2026  
**Supersedes:** Fire Safety Code v2 (2024)

To enhance venue egress safety during structural emergencies, all indoor assembly spaces must provide at least **1 unobstructed emergency exit per 75 attendees** (calculated as `ceil(attendance / 75)`).

Prior regulations allowing 1 exit per 100 attendees are fully superseded. Floor plans failing to meet this ratio must be rejected by safety coordinators prior to layout approval.
