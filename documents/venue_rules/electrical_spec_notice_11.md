# FACILITY ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING NOTICE: STAGE POWER CAPS
**Document ID:** ELECTRICAL-SPEC-NOTICE-11  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 5 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** Hall Electrical Specification v1

Following circuit breaker overloads and grid stabilization updates, total temporary stage power draw (lighting, audio amplifiers, and LED walls) in Hall B is strictly **capped at 10kW**.

Proposals exceeding 10kW must be rejected or re-engineered using low-draw energy-efficient stage equipment.
