# VENUE SECURITY LAYOUT & CAPACITY ADJUSTMENT SPECIFICATION
**Document ID:** SEC-LAYOUT-SPEC-01  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 5 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** Standard Venue V02 Floor Plan

When active screening stations (metal detectors and bag check lanes) are deployed at primary entrances, the usable floor area of Venue V02 is reduced by 15%.

Under these conditions, maximum permissible venue capacity is capped at **210 attendees**, overriding the static database venue capacity rating of 250.
