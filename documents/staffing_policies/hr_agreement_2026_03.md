# REVISED HR LABOR AGREEMENT: OVERTIME COMPENSATION
**Document ID:** HR-AGREEMENT-2026-03  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 1 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** Staffing Agreement v1 (2025)

Under the updated collective labor agreement, event staff working shifts exceeding **6 consecutive hours** must receive overtime compensation at **1.5x the base hourly rate** for all hours worked beyond the 6-hour threshold.

Prior policies allowing 8-hour shifts without overtime premium pay are null and void. Staffing budgets must explicitly calculate and log overtime cost line items for extended shifts.
