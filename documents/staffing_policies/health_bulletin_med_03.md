# PUBLIC HEALTH BULLETIN: GATHERING MEDICAL STANDARDS
**Document ID:** HEALTH-BULLETIN-MED-03  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 25 September 2026  
**Supersedes:** Standard Single-Medic Standby Policy

To ensure rapid emergency medical response at large-scale gatherings, assembly events must provide a minimum staffing ratio of **1 qualified paramedic per 100 attendees** (calculated as `ceil(attendance / 100)`).

A single medical standby is no longer sufficient for events exceeding 100 attendees.
