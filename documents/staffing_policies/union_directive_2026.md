# LABOR UNION DIRECTIVE: VOLUNTEER CAP RESTRICTIONS
**Document ID:** UNION-DIRECTIVE-2026  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 1 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** Volunteer Staffing Guidelines (40% limit)

To protect professional labor standards in paid corporate events, volunteer staffing utilization is strictly **capped at a maximum of 15%** of total personnel (rounded down).

For an event requiring 50 total roles, at least 43 must be paid professional staff.
