# HIGH-SECURITY DIRECTIVE: SUPERVISORY RATIOS
**Document ID:** HIGH-SEC-STAFF-02  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 10 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** General Staffing Ratio Standard (1:10)

For all events requiring heightened oversight, the mandatory supervisory hierarchy is updated to a strict **1 supervisor per 5 staff personnel** ratio (calculated as `ceil(total_staff / 5)`).

Legacy policies allowing 1 supervisor per 10 staff are superseded. Staffing budgets must account for increased supervisory headcounts.
