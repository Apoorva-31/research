# NOTICE OF TEMPORARY VENUE CAPACITY RESTRICTION
**Document ID:** NOTICE-VENUE-2026-03
**Status:** Current / Authoritative
**Effective Date:** 15 October 2026
**Supersedes:** Venue Policy v1 (June 2026)

Due to recent municipal structural safety inspections and load-bearing reassessments in Sector B, the maximum permitted attendance for all events in Venue V01 is strictly restricted to a maximum of **200 people**, overriding any prior baseline approvals of 240 or 250 attendees. 

All event coordinators and departmental leads must adjust seating and safety allocations immediately. Any plan exceeding 200 attendees will be deemed legally non-compliant.
