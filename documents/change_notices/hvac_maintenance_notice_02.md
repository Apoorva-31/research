# FACILITY MANAGEMENT NOTICE: COMPRESSED TEARDOWN WINDOWS
**Document ID:** HVAC-MAINTENANCE-NOTICE-02  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 18 September 2026  
**Supersedes:** Standard 1-Hour Post-Event Teardown Window

To accommodate scheduled overnight HVAC servicing and filter replacements, all event teardown operations must be completed within a compressed **30-minute window** following event conclusion (hard deadline: 21:30 for 21:00 event ends).

Catering and AV teardown crews must be scaled up to meet the accelerated timeline.
