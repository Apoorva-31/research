**Document ID:** CONSTRUCTION-NOTICE-PARKING-04  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 12 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** Primary Venue Parking Specification

Due to ongoing municipal repaving and utility trenching in Sector A parking bays, overall on-site vehicle parking capacity is **restricted to a maximum of 50 cars**, overriding the baseline 100-car capacity.

Logistics coordinators must arrange off-site overflow parking or mandatory attendee shuttle services.
