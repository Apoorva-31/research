# MUNICIPAL GREEN ORDINANCE: SINGLE-USE PLASTIC BAN
**Document ID:** GREEN-ORD-PLASTIC-BAN-2026  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 1 September 2026  
**Supersedes:** General Catering Utensil Guidelines

Effective immediately, the use of single-use plastic cups, plates, straws, and cutlery at public and corporate events is **strictly prohibited**.

Catering proposals must utilize 100% biodegradable, compostable, or reusable servingware to maintain municipal compliance.
