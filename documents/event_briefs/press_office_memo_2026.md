# PRESS OFFICE DIRECTIVE: MEDIA GALLERY ZONING
**Document ID:** PRESS-OFFICE-MEMO-2026  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 15 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** Open Seating Policy for Media

All press and accredited journalists covering corporate summits must be assigned to a designated, segregated **press gallery zone** equipped with dedicated power and audio feeds, separate from general attendees.

Security and seating plans must reflect this zone division.
