# HEALTH DEPARTMENT DIRECTIVE: OUTDOOR FOOD SERVICE
**Document ID:** HEALTH-DEPT-OUTDOOR-2026  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 1 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** Catering Service Manual (Section 3.1)

To ensure food hygiene compliance during outdoor lawn events, self-service open buffets are strictly prohibited. All food service for outdoor events must utilize individually **pre-packaged meals**.

Catering proposals incorporating open buffet lines for outdoor venues must be rejected by catering leads and resubmitted with packaged meal line-item unit pricing.
