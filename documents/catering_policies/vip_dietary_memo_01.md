# VIP DELEGATION MEMORANDUM: HALAL CATERING MANDATE
**Document ID:** VIP-DIETARY-MEMO-01  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 14 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** Standard VIP Catering Menu
