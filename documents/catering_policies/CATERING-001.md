# Version: 1.1
# Status: Current
# Effective Date: 1 July 2026

## Pricing & Requirements
- Standard catering unit cost is **₹450 per person**.
- Total meal quantities ordered must cover or exceed total confirmed attendance.
- Dietary breakdown: Every specified dietary requirement (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, allergen-free) must be explicitly covered out of the total meal count without shortchanging attendees.
