# FACILITY SAFETY DIRECTIVE: STRICT ALLERGEN CONTROL
**Document ID:** ALLERGEN-DIRECTIVE-2026  
**Status:** Current / Authoritative  
**Effective Date:** 1 October 2026  
**Supersedes:** General Snack and Catering Guidelines

Due to severe cross-contamination risks and guest health mandates, all corporate conferences and indoor catering events must maintain a **strict peanut-free and nut-free facility**. 

Catering teams must audit all snack selections, baked goods, and confectioneries to eliminate any peanut-derived ingredients prior to final menu approval.
